# Archetype: Fancy app

> Narrow scope, high polish. Weather, fitness, habit, meditation apps — they do a few jobs, but the *feel* is the differentiator. Here, delight is a feature, not a luxury.

## Who it's for

Consumers choosing on vibe and craft. They forgive limited features if the experience feels premium and alive.

## Must do ✅

- **Strong visual identity** — distinctive color, type, and imagery; a coherent theme.
- **Purposeful motion** — entrance, transitions, and micro-interactions that reward action.
- **Signature moments** — one or two memorable, delightful interactions.
- **Buttery performance** — 60fps, no jank; polish dies on stutter.
- **Detail everywhere** — empty states, loading, and edges all feel considered.

## Should avoid ❌

- ❌ Motion for its own sake that slows real use or ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- ❌ Style that sacrifices legibility or contrast.
- ❌ Polishing the happy path while empty/error states look unfinished.
- ❌ Feature creep that dilutes the focused concept.

## Notes from experience

> *(seed — to expand)* Pick **one** signature interaction to over-invest in; spread thin, polish reads as generic.
