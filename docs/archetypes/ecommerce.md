# Archetype: E-commerce / online shop

> Every screen serves one of three jobs: **find**, **decide**, **buy**. Friction or doubt anywhere leaks revenue.

## Who it's for

Shoppers — often mobile, often comparing, often skeptical. They abandon at the first sign of confusion, hidden cost, or distrust.

## Must do ✅

- **Fast, forgiving search & filtering** with clear results and good empty/no-results states.
- **Confident product pages** — great images, clear price, availability, and honest reviews.
- **Frictionless cart & checkout** — guest checkout, minimal fields, address autofill, multiple pay methods.
- **Total cost shown early** — shipping/taxes before the final step (no surprises).
- **Trust signals** — security cues, return policy, social proof, real support.
- **Instant feedback** on add-to-cart and every step.

## Should avoid ❌

- ❌ Forced account creation before buying.
- ❌ Surprise fees revealed at the last step.
- ❌ Long, multi-field forms with poor validation.
- ❌ Hidden stock/shipping info or fake-urgency dark patterns.
- ❌ Slow product images / layout shift while browsing.

## Notes from experience

> *(seed — to expand)* The two highest-impact fixes are usually **guest checkout** and **showing the full price before the last step**. Both directly attack the top reasons carts get abandoned.
