# Archetype: Game app

> *(New territory — early notes.)* Games live or die on **feel**: responsive, "juicy" feedback and a flow state where the next action is always clear and rewarding.

## Who it's for

Players seeking challenge, mastery, or relaxation. They tolerate complexity *if* it's taught gradually and the moment-to-moment feel is satisfying.

## Must do ✅ (early)

- **Juicy feedback** — every input produces immediate, exaggerated response (motion, sound, haptics, particles).
- **Teach by playing** — onboard through doing, not text walls; introduce mechanics one at a time.
- **Tuned difficulty curve** — easy to start, gradually harder; avoid early frustration and late boredom.
- **Clear goals & progress** — the player always knows what to do next and how they're doing.
- **Reward loops** — meaningful, well-paced rewards; respect the player's time.
- **Fast restart** — failing should drop you back into play instantly.

## Should avoid ❌ (early)

- ❌ Flat, unresponsive feedback that makes actions feel weightless.
- ❌ Tutorials that lecture instead of letting the player do.
- ❌ Difficulty spikes or unclear failure causes.
- ❌ Manipulative monetization / dark patterns that break trust.
- ❌ Long load times or interruptions that break flow.
